Sanctions screening

Screen a name against sanctions lists

Companies, organisations, vessels and aircraft on official lists, with an explanation of every match. Lists are downloaded when first used.

Check a vessel or aircraft identifier

Data: OpenSanctions (opensanctions.org) redistributes these official lists in a common format; licence CC BY-NC 4.0, non-commercial use only. Lists: US OFAC SDN, EU financial sanctions, UN Security Council, UK sanctions list, US consolidated screening list, Swiss SECO, US BIS denied persons. "First seen" is when OpenSanctions first saw the entry, which can lag the official listing date. Records a list does not classify as company or person are treated like persons. Natural persons are off by default and, when included, shown with name, list, programme and first-seen date only. A match is a lead to verify against the official list, not a finding; no match is not clearance. Interpol notices are not included.
