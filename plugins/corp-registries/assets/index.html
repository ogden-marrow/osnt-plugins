Company registries

Companies and who owns them

One search across open company registers. Company-level facts only; officers and shareholders who are individuals are not shown.

Sources: GLEIF (api.gleif.org, CC0), Wikidata (CC0), Norway Brønnøysund Register Centre (NLOD), Czech ARES (Ministry of Finance open data), Finnish PRH/YTJ open data (CC BY 4.0), Danish CVR via cvrapi.dk (non-commercial use), and, when you add keys on the Credentials page, UK Companies House, OpenCorporates and OCCRP Aleph under their own terms. Licences are as stated by each provider and were not independently verified here. Sole proprietors, sole traders and protected records are filtered out; owners, directors and shareholders are never requested. Registers differ in coverage and can be out of date; check the registry link before relying on a record.
