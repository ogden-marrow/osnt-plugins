Doppler flyby check

Check an aircraft or vehicle pass

Estimates speed, closest approach and distance from the Doppler shift in a recording, to test whether a clip's claimed aircraft, speed, place and time hold up. Analysed in memory, never stored. It does not locate or identify anyone.

Drop an audio clip of one pass or click to chooseWAV, MP3, M4A/AAC, OGG, FLAC. Up to 256 MB. A short clip of one pass is fitted directly; a long recording (up to two hours) is scanned for passes, which you can then open one by one.

Test a claim

No recording needed: enter a claimed pass and see what the Doppler physics predicts, for example a jet at 250 m/s passing 300 m away (900 km/h is 250 m/s).

Optional: candidate aircraft from recorded ADS-B history

Needs the adsb-history plugin. Gives candidates to check, never matches. Doppler has no heading, speed is relative to the air, and you only supply an approximate area. The archive is 10-second resolution, so speed and track are derived from successive samples. The first request can take a minute while the archive downloads.
