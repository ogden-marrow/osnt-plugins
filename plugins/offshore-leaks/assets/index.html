Offshore companies, trusts and foundations

Search ICIJ's Offshore Leaks database by entity name. Individuals are not searched.

Appearing here is not evidence of wrongdoing. Offshore structures have legitimate uses. The database is a set of leaked records; entries can be out of date (the Panama Papers data runs to 2015).

Data: International Consortium of Investigative Journalists (ICIJ), Offshore Leaks Database (Panama, Paradise and Pandora Papers, Bahamas Leaks, Offshore Leaks). ICIJ publishes the database under the Open Database License with contents under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike; see offshoreleaks.icij.org/terms-of-use (terms not verified by this plugin). Only entities and intermediaries are shown. Officer, beneficiary and nominee records, addresses, and the names of connected people are deliberately left out; open the ICIJ page for the full record. Queries and node pages are fetched on demand and cached.
