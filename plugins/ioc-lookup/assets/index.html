Submit a URL to urlscan.io (optional)

This sends the URL to a third party, which will visit it with its own browser and may keep the result. Pick "private" unless you want it public. Nothing is sent unless you press the button.

Sources: urlscan.io search (public scans; their terms apply), AlienVault OTX, GreyNoise Community API (non-commercial use), mnemonic passive DNS (public, limited), Shodan InternetDB (free for non-commercial use), and with your own keys VirusTotal and AbuseIPDB (each under its own terms). Results are cached for 15 minutes. Verdict wording belongs to each source; this page does not combine them. Domain and URL indicators can belong to private websites: use this for infrastructure, not to profile people.
