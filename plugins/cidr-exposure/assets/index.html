Netblock exposure

What does a netblock expose?

Open ports, products and known CVEs per address, read from Shodan InternetDB's existing data. Completely passive: nothing is sent to the addresses.

Enter a block

Blocks up to the configured limit are expanded and looked up one address at a time at a polite rate. Results are cached for six hours, so repeating a scan is quick.

Data: Shodan InternetDB, a free API for non-commercial use (see Shodan's terms); it reports what Shodan last observed, which can be days old, and omits addresses it never indexed. No port scanning is done by this tool. Use it on networks you own, administer or have a legitimate reason to assess.
