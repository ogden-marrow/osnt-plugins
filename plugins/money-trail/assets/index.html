Sources: USAspending.gov (US Treasury; public domain), ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer (IRS Form 990 data; ProPublica terms apply), US Senate/House Lobbying Disclosure Act database (lda.gov; public), and EU Tenders Electronic Daily via the TED API (Publications Office of the EU; reuse with attribution). Amounts are as reported by each source; award values are obligations, not payments. Name matching is by text, so related companies and unrelated namesakes may appear: check the matched names. Recipients are restricted to business, government, higher-education and nonprofit categories; nonprofit pages omit care-of names and street addresses; lobbying rows omit lobbyist and contact names.
