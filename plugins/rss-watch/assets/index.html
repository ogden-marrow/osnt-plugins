About the data. Items are fetched directly from the feeds you add and stored here for 30 days (up to 150 per feed); text belongs to its publishers and is shown as a short summary with a link. Hacker News search is provided by Algolia's public HN API. Telegram previews read only the public t.me/s/<channel> page of a channel you name: post text, time and link. Groups, members and users are not supported, and nobody's profile is read.
