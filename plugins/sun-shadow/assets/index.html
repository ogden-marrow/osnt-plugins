Enter the time shown in the photo's place; the offset converts it to UTC (Paris in summer: 2). Guess from longitude. Click the map or drag the pin to move.

Uses the pin and the date above. A shadow of one length happens once in the morning and once in the afternoon.

Needs the exact date and time converted to UTC. The pin is ignored: the band shows every place with that sun height.
